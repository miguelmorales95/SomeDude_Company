# Kinmap printed keepsakes: LA-area print partners and online POD baselines

**As of 2026-11-01.** Research for a solo founder (South Bay / LA County) selling hardcover family-tree photo books (20-60 pp, ~8x8 to 11x8.5), wall posters (18x24, 24x36), coil family binders and wall calendars at 5-50 orders/month, shipped across the US (mostly California).

## How to read this

- **Verified** = read on the company's own site (or the vendor's own pricing page) during this research.
- **Directory / secondary** = a third-party listing, trade article or directory page. Treat as a lead only.
- **Not published / quote needed** = no price found. No prices were invented.
- Several company sites blocked automated fetches (403, JavaScript-only pages, timeouts: Gelato, WHCC, Typecraft, Harman Press, Sir Speedy, Miller's). I did not work around bot protection, so those entries rest on secondary sources and need a phone call.
- Consumer retail prices (Mixbook, Mpix, etc.) are shown only as a ceiling for what a family would see elsewhere. They are not wholesale costs.
- No LA-area shop found publishes prices for hardcover photo books or posters. Every LA price is **quote needed**.

## 3. Short answers

### (1) In-house vs partner at 5-50 orders/month (opinion, based on the above)

- **Hardcover books: partner.** Case binding is not a home job, and short-run digital case-binders cost five figures. Use an API (RPI/Lulu/Prodigi) for the base product and a local bindery or Axiom for premium or rush orders.
- **Posters: partner at first, in-house only later.** At 5-50 a month, a drop-ship or local giclee partner avoids tube packing, ink, paper waste and calibration time for someone with a day job. Consider in-house only after about 25-30 poster orders per month, if the best partner price is clearly above your ink, media, tube and time cost.
- **Coil binders: the one plausible in-house product**, but only for small, reorder-light volumes, because it still needs a laser or inkjet for the pages plus cover stock. At 5-50 orders, a partner (Lulu coil bound, Axiom or Minuteman) is lower-risk. Revisit if coil binders become a sizeable share of orders.
- **Calendars: partner** (Lulu wire-o, Bay Photo press calendars, or a local shop). Seasonal peaks would overwhelm one person.

## 4. Suggested next steps

1. Phone/email: Harman Press (confirm status, wholesale account), Axiom (sample book, 5/10/25 copy quote), Kater-Crafts (casing in 10-50 digitally printed books), Typecraft.
2. Free accounts: RPI Print, Prodigi, Lulu. Order one 20-page 8x8 hardcover from each (or the nearest size) for a quality and cost test, plus a calendar from Lulu.
3. Visit The Copy Shop (Manhattan Beach) or Harman Press with a test poster file at 18x24 and 24x36.
4. Keep Kinmap's policy in mind when choosing partners: no resale of family data, protected files, and deletion of print files after fulfillment. Ask each partner how long files and addresses are retained.
