Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026.

SomeDude is a small independent studio run by one developer, Miguel Morales. This page covers the somedude.app website. Each app has its own privacy policy, because each one handles different information and keeps it separately: the apps don't share accounts or data with each other.

This website

  • It has no sign-in, forms, ads, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • It's hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running. We don't use them to profile anyone.
  • The page loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details when the font is fetched.
  • If you email hello@somedude.app, we use your message and address only to reply.

The apps

  • Kinmap (family trees): kinmap.somedude.app/privacy
  • Afoot (local events): afoot.somedude.app/privacy
  • NoBucks (neighborhood time bank): its policy is shown in the app, and will be posted here when it opens to the public.

What we never do

  • Sell your information, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Show targeted ads based on what you do in our apps.

Questions

Email hello@somedude.app. If you'd like us to delete anything you've sent us, ask and we will.
