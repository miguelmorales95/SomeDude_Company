How we work

Privacy first

No tracking ads and no selling data, in any of our apps. Each app keeps its own data.

Paid for honestly

Subscriptions, local sponsors and grants: each app earns in a way that fits the people who use it.

Made by one person

SomeDude is an independent studio run by Miguel Morales, a solo developer building careful, sustainable products.

Built for everyone

Readable, accessible, and available in the languages families actually speak.
